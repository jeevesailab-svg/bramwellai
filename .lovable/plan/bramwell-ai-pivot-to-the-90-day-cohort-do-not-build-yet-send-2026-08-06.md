## 7. The Mirror Moment

Cosmetic companies understand something most B2B brands don't: **people buy when they confront the gap between who they are and who they want to be.**

The cosmetic company's mirror moment is the woman looking at her reflection and seeing fine lines, dull skin, the version of herself she doesn't want to be. The product promises a transformation she can see.

The weight loss company's mirror moment is the "before" photo. The number on the scale. The jeans that don't fit. The moment she says "this has to change."

Bramwell's mirror moment is the Readiness Score.

Sarah takes a 5-minute voice test. She gets a number: 42 out of 100. And in that number, she sees everything she's been feeling — every meeting she shrank in, every presentation she cancelled, every time she watched someone less qualified get heard instead of her.

The number makes the invisible visible. Just like the mirror.

Vocal Image uses this same mechanic: "Take the test" and "Don't like your own voice?" are their entry points. RiseGuide uses "Start with a test." All three competitors understand: **the quiz is the hook. The score is the mirror. The transformation is the sale.**

**This is why the diagnostic is the free tier.** Not because free drives signups. Because the mirror moment drives transformation. You can't sell a solution to someone who doesn't believe they have a problem. The score makes them believe.

## Voice rules

1. **Never start with the technology.** Start with the feeling. "Your voice is already costing you opportunities" — not "AI-powered voice analysis."
2. **Never use jargon.** "Sound confident in meetings" — not "optimise your verbal communication metrics."
3. **Never beg.** No "Sign up now!" or "Limited time!" or "Don't miss out!" Premium brands don't beg. They invite.
